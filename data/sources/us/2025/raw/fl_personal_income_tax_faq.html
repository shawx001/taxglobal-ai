Do I have to file a personal income tax return in Florida?

Florida does not impose a personal income tax, so there are no filing requirements. However, there are filing requirements for businesses.

Learn more about taxes your business may have to pay in the brochure Considering Business Opportunities in Florida? (GT-800029).

You may also find it helpful to read the brochure Tax Information for New Residents (GT-800025​) to find out about taxes ​you may have to pay as a Florida resident.

For information about federal income tax, visit the Internal Revenue Service website or call (800) 829-1040.
