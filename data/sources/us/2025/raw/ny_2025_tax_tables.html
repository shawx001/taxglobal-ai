2025 tax tables

  1. Select the return you file:
    • Form IT-201, Resident Income Tax Return, for full-year residents of New York State, or
    • Form IT-203, Nonresident and Part-Year Resident Income Tax Return, for nonresidents and part-year residents of New York State.
  2. View the information on where to find the tax rates and tables for:
    • New York State,
    • New York City,
    • Yonkers, and
    • the metropolitan commuter transportation mobility tax (MCTMT).

Forms

IT-201 filers

New York State Tax
If your: And Then use:
NYS adjusted gross income is $107,650 or less (line 33) NYS taxable income is less than $65,000 (line 38) NYS tax table
NYS taxable income is $65,000 or more (line 38) NYS tax rate schedule
NYS adjusted gross income is more than $107,650 (line 33) NYS tax computation

 

New York City Resident Tax
If: Then use:
Your NYC taxable income is less than $65,000 (line 47) NYC tax table
Your NYC taxable income is $65,000 or more (line 47) NYC tax rate schedule
You are a part-year NYC resident Form IT-360.1 and its instructions
You are married and filing a joint NYS return and one spouse was a full-year New York City resident and the other was a nonresident for all of the tax year Form IT-201 instructions for line 51

 

Yonkers Tax
If you are a: Then:
Yonkers full year resident See Form IT-201 instructions for line 55
Yonkers nonresident and had earnings in Yonkers Use Form Y-203 and its instructions
Yonkers part-year resident Use Form IT-360.1 and its instructions

 

Metropolitan commuter transportation mobility tax (MCTMT)
If you are subject to: And: Then:
MCTMT Net earnings from self-employment allocated to either zone of the MCTD exceeding $50,000 See Form IT-201 instructions lines 54a and 54b

IT-203 filers

New York State Tax
If your: And Then use:
NYS adjusted gross income is $107,650 or less (line 32) NYS taxable income is less than $65,000 (line 37) NYS tax table
NYS taxable income is $65,000 or more (line 37) NYS tax rate schedule
NYS adjusted gross income is more than $107,650 (line 32) NYS tax computation

 

New York City or Yonkers Tax
If you are a: Then:
Part-year NYC resident Use Form IT-360.1 and its instructions
Yonkers nonresident and had earnings in Yonkers Use Form Y-203 and its instructions
Part-year Yonkers resident Use Form IT-360.1 and its instructions

 

Metropolitan commuter transportation mobility tax (MCTMT)
If you are subject to: And: Then:
MCTMT Net earnings from self-employment allocated to either zone of the MCTD exceeding $50,000 See Form IT-203 instructions for lines 52b and 52c
